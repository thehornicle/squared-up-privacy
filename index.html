Squared Up: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Squared Up is an app for splitting a bill between friends. This page explains what it does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What the app does with your information

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
  • No data collection. Squared Up does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. We do not know who you are, who you split bills with, or what you buy.
  • Everything stays on your phone. The names you add, the receipts, the amounts and the results are kept only on your device, inside the app. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it.
  • No internet needed. The app makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Camera and photos

If you choose "Take photo", the app asks for permission to use your camera, so you can photograph a receipt. If you choose "Upload photo", you pick a picture from your photo library using the standard iOS picker; the app only receives the one picture you choose.

The text on the receipt is read on your phone, using Apple's built-in text recognition. The picture is not uploaded, saved by the app, or sent anywhere, and it is discarded once the text has been read.

Sharing

The "Copy summary" button copies a short text summary to your clipboard so you can paste it into a message. Whether to send it, and to whom, is entirely up to you. The app does not send it anywhere itself.

The "Share with the group" button makes a link that you can send with Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop or any other app. The breakdown (names, items and amounts) is packed inside the link itself, after the "#" sign. When a friend opens it, their browser shows a read-only page from our website, but browsers never send the part after "#" to any server, so the breakdown is not uploaded or stored anywhere and we cannot see it. Anyone who has the link can see the breakdown, so only send it to the people involved.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
